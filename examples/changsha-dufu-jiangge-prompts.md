## Front

```text
Create a landscape 3:2 illustrated postcard front on warm ivory rice paper with fine visible grain, in an elegant Chinese "city in poetry" style.

LEFT (about 60% of the width): repaint the main subject of the reference photo — a three-tier Chinese pavilion with sweeping upturned eaves, white stone balustrades and stairs at the base — as a refined ink-and-watercolor / gouache illustration. Keep the same camera angle as the photo: low angle, close, looking steeply up at the front-left corner, with the eaves soaring upward. Keep the silhouette, proportions, roof lines, number of tiers, plaque position and structural details faithful to the photo. Lighting: night, warm floodlit gold eaves and balconies against a dark sky. Fine ink linework on the architecture, warm lantern gold highlights. Sky as loose dry-brush deep indigo ink-wash strokes with a thin crescent moon. Complete the scene with restrained dark green-black trees at both sides and white stone stairs in the foreground. The painting fades into the paper with ragged dry-brush vignette edges; no rectangular frame. Omit visitors or keep them tiny and indistinct; no identifiable faces.

SIGNAGE: the dark plaque under the top eave reads "杜甫江阁" from right to left (杜 on the far right, 阁 on the far left) — paint these exact characters in this exact order, or leave the plaque blank if they cannot be rendered cleanly. No other readable text inside the painting.

RIGHT (about 40% of the width), on open paper:
- large expressive brush-calligraphy title: "杜甫江阁" in dark ink blue
- below it, small widely spaced serif capitals: "DU FU JIANG GE"
- one small vermilion seal to the right of the subtitle reading "长沙"
- two lines of verse in elegant Kai/Song style: "夜醉长沙酒，" / "晓行湘水春。"
- right-aligned attribution: "—— 杜甫《发潭州》"

CORNERS, small and thin:
- top-left: "CHANGSHA", a short rule, then "长沙" set vertically
- top-right: "A CITY IN POETRY" as stacked small capitals with a short rule below
- bottom-right: "湘江潮落 · 诗意长留" in small text

All text must be spelled exactly as given, character for character. Limited palette: warm lantern gold, deep indigo ink blue, deep green-black and warm umber, ivory paper, one vermilion seal.

Do not embed the photo itself, add a frame, color swatches, extra seals, logos, badges, extra text, or a translation of the poem.

Quality: highest detail, crisp ink lines, clean watercolor without mud, refined paper texture, low noise, largest available 3:2 resolution, suitable for later 4x upscaling.

Mood: quiet, nocturnal, softly glowing, collectible.
```

## Back

```text
Create a matching landscape 3:2 postcard back on the same warm ivory rice paper, with the same thin ink-blue line style as the front.

Keep it functional and mostly blank:
- thin outer border
- one vertical divider slightly right of center
- stamp box in the upper-right
- 4 address lines on the right
- large blank message area on the left

Optionally: small "POST CARD · 明信片" near the upper-left; a very faint ink-wash echo of the 杜甫江阁 pavilion silhouette along the lower-left edge, low enough not to block writing; tiny "杜甫江阁 · 长沙" near the lower-left.

Do not add the poem, a painting, extra seals, or decoration that reduces writing space.

Quality: clean sharp lines, refined paper texture, low noise, largest available 3:2 resolution, suitable for later 4x upscaling.
```
